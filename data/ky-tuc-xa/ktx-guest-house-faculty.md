# Guest house policy for visiting lecturers

## Eligibility

Visiting lecturers with a teaching contract of at least one month may book the guest house. Student dormitory rooms are not available to faculty guests.

## Booking procedure

Send a booking request by email at least 14 days before arrival. The management office confirms availability within 3 working days.

## Rates and checkout

The guest house rate is 300000 dong per night. Checkout time is 12 o'clock noon. Guests staying more than 30 nights receive a 10 percent discount.
